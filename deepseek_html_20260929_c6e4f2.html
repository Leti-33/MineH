Why this matters

Addressing South Africa's mining safety challenges with proactive IoT and edge intelligence.

Health & environmental fusion

Continuous monitoring of blood oxygen, heart rate, body temperature, and toxic gases. Alerts when vitals or air quality cross safe thresholds.

  • MAX30102 + MLX90614 (body temp)
  • MQ135 & DHT11 for environment
  • Piezoelectric impact detection

Through-the-earth & cloud

ESP32 handles edge processing and transmits via Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or TTE communication — reaching depths of several kilometres.

  • NEO 7M GPS for surface tracking
  • Wristband with redundant buzzers
  • Cloud dashboard & predictive alerts
